Our story

A husband, a wife and a lot of websites

Chris spent ten years building databases for a living. Leah grew up on Walpole Island. These days we spend our evenings calling small businesses, and we kept hearing the same thing: their website didn’t do them justice, or they had no website at all.

So we flipped the usual deal. We do the work first. You see your new site before you pay, and if it’s not right, you walk away owing nothing.

— Chris & Leah Courteaux

Kitchen-table questions

Things people ask us most

What if I don’t like it?

Then you owe nothing. Tell us what’s off and we’ll try again, or walk away. The preview is free either way.

What do I need to send?

Just a link: your website, your Facebook page or your Google listing. We gather your photos, colours and words from there.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer, only after you’ve picked a design. Custom sites start at $100, and it’s 33% off if you don’t need a demo.

Can I change it later?

Yes. Ask us, or open your site in BAKE Studio and change it yourself.
