# BAKE call script (prices updated Nov 19, 2026)

**Who we are on the phone:** RezSite. The call starts as a quick check of their RezSite directory listing, then offers **3 free website designs**. BAKE is RezSite's web design studio.

**Your goal on every call:** (1) confirm the listing, (2) get a yes to the free designs, (3) get the email to send them to. You're booking the free designs. Chris builds them while you keep calling.

**Turnaround:** yes before **3:00 pm ET** → designs **today**. Yes after 3:00 → **tomorrow by noon**.

---

## 1. Opening: listing check (everyone)
> "Hi, this is **[your name]** calling from **RezSite**. We run an Indigenous-owned business directory, and **[business]** is listed with us. I'm just calling to make sure your details are right. Is this a good number for you, and is **[website]** still your website?"

If they're not the owner or manager:
> "Who would be the best person to talk to about your website? … Is there a good time to reach them?"

Check quickly, and write any corrections in **Notes**: phone, website, town, and what the business does (one line).

## 2. The offer (right after the check)
> "Thanks, that's all up to date. One more thing, and it's free. We also have a design studio called **BAKE**, and this week we're making **three free website designs** for businesses in the directory, built around your business, your logo and your photos. No cost, no obligation, and you'd have them **[turnaround]**. Would you like us to make yours?"

**For pitch-ready businesses (designs already made):**
> "…and actually, we've already made three designs for **[business]**. Can I email or text you the link right now so you can look while we're on the phone?"

Then stay on the line while they open it: "What do you think of the first one?"

## 3. If yes: the checklist (fill in the form)
1. **Contact person:** "Who should the designs go to?" (name + role)
2. **Email:** "What's the best email to send them to? Is it OK if we email you the link?" Read it back letter by letter. *(Their yes is our permission to email. Write it down.)*
3. **Their current site:** "We'll use the photos, videos and information from your current site, so there's nothing you need to send us. Is there anything you'd like changed or added, like new photos or another page?" *(Write anything they'd like in Notes.)*

Close:
> "Perfect. You'll have three designs **[turnaround]**. And just so you know: when we email them to you, any design you pick in the **first 24 hours is half price**, then 40% off the next day, and 10% less each day after that. Thanks, **[their name]**!"

Then message **Chris right away** with the business name.

## 4. Voicemail (under 20 seconds)
> "Hi, this is **[your name]** from **RezSite**, the Indigenous business directory. **[business]** is listed with us. I'm calling to confirm your details and to offer you three free website designs this week. I'll try you again later today. Thanks!"

## Price and what's included (only when they ask)
> "The designs are free. If you like one, it's a **one-time price, usually $50 to $250 (Canadian)** depending on the design, with **no monthly fees**. And when we email the designs, it's **50% off for the first 24 hours**, then 40% off the next day, then 10% less each day."

| They ask about | You say |
|---|---|
| **Hosting** | "Hosting is **free**, on your free address **or on your own domain**, with a small 'Baked with BAKE' badge at the bottom of your pages. If you'd rather not have it, it's a small monthly fee based on how busy your site is, **starting at $5 a month**." |
| **Address** | "You get a **free address** like *yourbusiness.rezsite.com* or *yourbusiness.bakedby.me*." |
| **Their own domain** | "We can connect the domain you already have for **$100 one-time**." |
| **A new domain** | "We can register a new one for you for **$50**, plus the yearly domain fee, usually **about $15 a year**." |
| **Bigger firms** | "For a bigger site, Chris quotes it after you've seen the designs, and the half-price offer still applies." |
| **Paying** | "By Interac e-Transfer. Your designs link has a **Buy** section: pick a name, you get a pay code, send the e-Transfer with that code, and your site goes live on rezsite.com as soon as it arrives." |

## Common objections

| They say | You say |
|---|---|
| **"We already have a website."** | "That's why we called. We'll show you what a fresh version could look like. If you like yours better, nothing changes. It's free." |
| **"What's the catch?"** | "No catch. We make the designs, you look at them. If you don't love one, just say no thanks." |
| **"I'm busy."** | "No problem. It takes a minute: I just need an email. Or when's a better time?" (Write the time in **Next step**.) |
| **"Just send me some information."** | "The best information is the designs themselves, made for you. What email should they go to?" |
| **"We have someone who does our website."** | "Great, they can look too. Three free designs might give you both some ideas." |
| **"I need to talk to my partner / board."** | "Of course. The designs make that easy. Can I send them so you can share them?" |
| **"How did you get my number?"** | "You're listed in the RezSite business directory, from public listings. I can remove you if you'd like." |
| **"Take us out of the directory."** | "Done, I'll make sure that's removed." (Write it in **Notes**; outcome *Do not call again*.) |
| **"Not interested."** | "No problem, thanks for your time." (Don't push.) |
| **"Don't call again."** | "Understood, I'll take you off our list." (Outcome *Do not call again*.) |
| **A question you can't answer** | "Good question. I'll have Chris get back to you today." (Write it in **Next step**.) |

## Never say
- Don't say a business is Indigenous-owned unless they tell you.
- Don't promise prices or features beyond this page.
- Don't promise the sale before the designs are emailed: it starts when Chris emails them.
- Don't email anyone who didn't give you their email on the call (or publish it on their own site).
