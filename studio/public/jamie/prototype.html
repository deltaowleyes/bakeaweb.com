Questions families ask

How long does a custom headstone take?

Most memorials are ready 8–12 weeks after you approve your proof. Cemeteries also have seasonal setting schedules, so we'll confirm your installation date with them.

Can I put a photo on a granite headstone?

Yes. Choose a laser-etched portrait, which looks best on black granite, or a sealed colour porcelain photo that works on any colour.

Do you deliver across Canada?

Yes. We offer local delivery and setting, freight delivery to your cemetery or monument dealer anywhere in Canada, or pickup.

What if the cemetery has rules about size or shape?

Many do. Tell us the cemetery at checkout and we'll check its bylaws before we send your proof.

What if I spot a mistake after I submit?

Nothing is engraved until you approve the final proof. You can ask for as many changes as you need before then.

The name I'd suggest: Stone & Light Memorials

Stone is the granite and Light is the crystals, so one brand covers both sides of the business. It sounds calm and respectful, and it works as a domain (stoneandlight.ca). Some other options:

Try it in about 3 minutes

  1. Open Design a Memorial. Change the shape and granite, click a name to edit it, drag things around, and upload a family photo (drag it onto the stone or use “Add photo”).
  2. Click Preview proof, tick the spelling check, and add it to the cart.
  3. On 3D Photo Crystals, upload a photo to see a quick 3D preview, then add one to the cart.
  4. In the Cart, change the province to see GST/HST/PST and delivery update, then place a demo order.
  5. Open Jamie's Proof Desk. Send the proof, click “Customer requests changes”, revise it in the designer, resend it, approve it, and move it into production.

The Google (SEO) plan

  • Google Business Profile set up and verified, with photos of real finished memorials. This is what shows up on Google Maps.
  • A page for each search people make, such as “custom granite headstones”, “headstone with photo”, “heart-shaped headstone”, “flat grave markers”, “3D photo crystal Canada”, and city pages for your service area.
  • Business details for Google (the Store and Product markup already in this code), so prices and reviews can show in search results.
  • Fast pages that work well on phones. Most of these searches happen on a phone, often in a hard week for the family.
  • Helpful guides, such as cemetery rules, how long a headstone takes and choosing granite colours. They bring in visitors and build trust.
  • Reviews: after each installation we automatically ask the customer to leave a Google review.

  • Your delivery area and how you handle installation
  • The name of your crystal supplier, and whether they have an API or partner program
  • The name you like, and a logo if you have one (if not, we can make one)
