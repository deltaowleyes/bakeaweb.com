Questions families ask

How does the proof work?

After you order a granite memorial, our team checks spelling, spacing and your cemetery's rules, then emails you a digital proof. You can approve it or ask for changes. Nothing is engraved until you approve.

How long does a custom granite memorial take?

Most granite memorials are ready 8–12 weeks after you approve your proof. Crystals usually ship within 5–7 business days. (Sample timelines, set by True North.)

Can I make a memorial for my pet?

Yes. Heart and flat markers are popular for pets, and a crystal with your pet's photo makes a beautiful keepsake for the home.

Do you ship across Canada?

Yes. Crystals ship by tracked courier. Granite memorials can be delivered by freight to your home, cemetery or monument dealer anywhere in Canada, or picked up.

What if the cemetery has rules about size or shape?

Many do. Tell us the cemetery at checkout and we'll check its rules before we send your proof.

Try it in 3 minutes

  1. On Crystal Memorials, pick a crystal, upload a photo, then click Personalize in 3D to see the hand-off to your supplier.
  2. On Granite Memorials, click Design this on the heart. Change the granite, click a name to edit it, and drop a family or pet photo onto the stone.
  3. Click Preview proof, add it to the cart, change the province to see taxes and delivery update, and place a demo order.
  4. Open the Proof Desk. Send the proof, click "Customer requests changes", revise it, then approve it and move it into production.
  5. Open the Store Manager. Change a price or the home page headline, press Save, then look at the shop.
