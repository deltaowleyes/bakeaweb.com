How to read the three levels

  • Unaligned investor — what an outside investor with no stake in us, no loyalty and no interest in our story would pay today. It starts at Rob's figure of about $50K and only moves for things that investor can check: working product, a call list that converts, real buyers, real money. Ideas, plans and AI grades move it very little. This line is Claude's model anchored to Rob's starting number; Rob did not give the daily figures.
  • Realistic — Claude's blended estimate (cost to rebuild + early-stage scorecard + forward revenue), cross-checked against Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity and ChatGPT on Nov 14. It's what a friendly early investor might accept in a negotiation, not a price anyone has offered.
  • Berkus ceiling — the Berkus pre-revenue method: up to $500K each for idea, prototype, team, relationships and sales. It's a pitching method and the most generous of the three. Use it as the upper limit, never as the headline.
  • All figures are USD. The Nov 13 figure was given in CAD (C$75K–300K) and is converted at 1.40. These are informal estimates, not professional valuations or financial advice. None of them is revenue: as of Nov 20 we have $0 in sales, so the first paid e-Transfer is the single biggest move available to every line.
