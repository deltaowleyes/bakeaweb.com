# Plan: Names and trademarks check

Task id: `names-and-trademarks-check` · Written 2026-11-08 · Planning only (no code)

> **This is not legal advice.** It is research to help you have a short, cheap, well-prepared conversation with a Canadian trademark lawyer or trademark agent. Only they can give you a final answer.

## The short version

| Name | Risk | Why, in one line |
|---|---|---|
| **IndigiHub** (indigihub.cloud) | 🔴 **High** | A well-known, award-nominated Indigenous business platform called IndigiHub already runs at indigihub.com, for the same people, and plans "First Nations community portals" that overlap with ours. |
| **Bake a Web** (bakeaweb.com) | 🟠 **Medium** | A website builder called **BakeMyWeb** already exists. The names sound alike and sell the same thing. |
| **BAKE** (the brand) | 🟡 **Low to medium** | Lots of "Bake" food trademarks in Canada (no real problem), but several new **software** "BAKE" filings in the United States. Fine in Canada for now; matters if we grow into the US. |
| **Baked by me** (bakedby.me) | 🟢 **Low** | Thousands of bakeries use "Baked by…" names, but for cakes, not websites. Watch the cannabis slang. |
| **Baked by a pro** (bakedbya.pro) | 🟢 **Low** | Only cookbooks with "bake like a pro" titles turned up. Nothing in websites. |

**My recommendation (for you to decide):** keep BAKE, Baked by me and Baked by a pro; ask a lawyer about Bake a Web; and **don't launch "IndigiHub" under that name**. Instead, reach out to IndigiHub's founder first (email draft below). Either we partner with them and use the name together, with their blessing, or we pick a new name for our free Indigenous pages before anything goes public.

## 2. IndigiHub: who they are

- **What it is:** IndigiHub (https://indigihub.com/) is a platform that helps Indigenous entrepreneurs across Canada find funding, business resources and opportunities in one place. Its site has sections for **Funding, Resources, Tools and a Directory**.
- **Who runs it:** it was founded by **Zachary (Nelson) Flett**, a member of **Sagkeeng First Nation**, in Manitoba. It describes itself as a growing national platform for Indigenous entrepreneurship.
- **What else they do:** in-person networking nights called **Neechie Nights** in Winnipeg, alongside the online platform.
- **Recognition:** a 2026 profile says IndigiHub received the **Manitoba Tech Award for Community Impact** in 2026. It was also a finalist for the **Indigenous Innovation Award** at North Forge's 2026 DARE Innovation Awards, and Zachary was shortlisted for the Ecosystem Builder Award. Other Indigenous business groups (for example the Northeastern Alberta Aboriginal Business Association) already list IndigiHub as a resource.
- **Where they're heading (the important part):** the same profile says they are building **AI business-planning tools, personalised funding recommendations, pitch-deck creation and First Nations community portals**.

**Why that matters to us:** our IndigiHub is free web pages *and a directory* for Indigenous people, businesses and communities. Theirs is a platform, a directory and soon *community portals* for the same people. To a visitor, `indigihub.cloud` and `indigihub.com` would look like the same organisation. That is the textbook situation trademark law calls **"confusion"**, and it doesn't matter much whether either side has filed a trademark: in Canada, a business that has been *using* a name (like they have, for years, with awards and press) can have rights in it even without registration ("passing off").

It's also a relationship question, not just a legal one. A non-Indigenous company launching a same-named Indigenous platform next to an Indigenous founder's award-winning one would be seen as harmful, whatever the intent. Getting this right is part of earning trust with the communities IndigiHub is meant to serve.

## 3. The other names

### Bake a Web (bakeaweb.com)
- **BakeMyWeb** (by Attosol) is a cloud website builder for making, launching and managing pages and blog posts with no coding. "Bake a Web" and "Bake My Web" are very close in sound and idea, and both are website builders.
- I don't know whether BakeMyWeb holds a Canadian or US trademark. **Ask the lawyer.** If they do, a lawyer might suggest leaning on **"BAKE Studio"** as the main brand, with bakeaweb.com only as the address.
- "The Baker" is also a small developer project that "bakes" website clones; tiny, not a concern.

### BAKE
- **In Canada:** I found many CIPO records with "BAKE" in them, all for food or bakeries (for example *Bisou Bake House*, *Dao Bake & Sip Café*, *Bake to Rise* frozen pizzas). Trademarks are tied to what they're used for, so bakery marks are unlikely to block a website studio. "BAKE" alone is a common word, which makes it weaker for everyone, including us.
- **In the US (software, Class 42):** recent filings include **BAKE** by Cake Pte Ltd (registered 2024, finance/business software), **BAKE** by Honeycake, Inc. (filed March 2026, file-encryption software), and **BAKE AI** / **BAKELAB** by Bake AI Inc. (filed November 2025). None is a website builder, but they show "BAKE" is getting crowded in software. Matters if we sell in the US later.

### Baked by me (bakedby.me) and Baked by a pro (bakedbya.pro)
- "Baked by [name]" is everywhere for real bakeries (*Baked by Melissa*, a US cupcake chain, and countless home bakeries). Different business, low risk for a website host.
- "Bake like a pro" only appears as book titles.
- **Heads-up:** "baked" is common cannabis slang in North America, and Canadian cannabis brands use baking words too (for example *Bake Sale* by HEXO). Not a legal risk, but avoid slogans that lean into it, and expect the odd joke.

Can be done before launch at almost no cost. | Lose the name you like; need a new domain (about $10–$40 a year). |

A sensible order: **try A first**; if there's no reply in about 3 weeks, or they'd rather not, **switch to B** before the IndigiHub pages go public. The free hosting setup can keep running in the meantime as long as indigihub.cloud isn't promoted.

If we rename, the code change is small (the version name in `studio/src/editions.js`, `VISION.md`, the domain). That would be its own BAKE task.

## 6. Check CIPO yourself (5 minutes, free)

1. Open the Canadian Trademarks Database: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch
2. In the search box type **INDIGIHUB** and press **Search**. Then try **INDIGI HUB** (with a space) and **INDIGI**.
3. Repeat for **BAKE A WEB**, **BAKEAWEB**, **BAKE MY WEB**, **BAKED BY ME**, **BAKED BY A PRO**, **BAKE STUDIO**.
4. For any result, click it and note: the owner, the status (*Registered*, *Advertised*, *Formalized* = still being examined), and the **Nice classes**. The ones that matter for us are **35** (advertising, online directories and marketplaces), **42** (software and website building) and **45** in some cases. Ignore **30** (bread, cakes).
5. Send the list to the lawyer along with this file.

## 7. Questions for a lawyer or trademark agent

Find one through the College of Patent Agents and Trademark Agents directory (https://cpata-cabamc.ca/) or ask about free help through an IP advisor (https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canadian-intellectual-property-office/en). A basic search-and-opinion often costs a few hundred to a couple of thousand dollars; ask for a fixed price. CIPO's own filing fee is a few hundred dollars per mark (check CIPO's fee page for the current amount).

1. Does IndigiHub (indigihub.com) have unregistered rights in the name from use, and would our `indigihub.cloud` for Indigenous free websites be "confusing" with it under Canada's *Trademarks Act* or passing-off law?
2. If we partner, what should a simple name-licence or co-branding agreement include (who owns the name, what happens if the partnership ends)?
3. Is "Bake a Web" / bakeaweb.com too close to "BakeMyWeb"? Does BakeMyWeb hold rights in Canada?
4. Can we register **BAKE** for website-building software and hosting (classes 9, 35, 42) given it's a common word with lots of bakery marks? Would **BAKE STUDIO** or a logo be stronger?
5. Are "Baked by me" and "Baked by a pro" registrable, or too descriptive? Is it worth registering them at all?
6. Which names should we file first, and should we file in the US too (given the recent US "BAKE" software filings)?
7. Do we need to worry about the cannabis association of "Baked" under Canadian cannabis advertising rules if a cannabis business makes a site with us?
8. For any rename of the Indigenous version: how should we clear a new name, and should Indigenous-language words be avoided or used only with community permission?
