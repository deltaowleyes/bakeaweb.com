## Talk 1 (2026-11-08): versions and accounts, stopped and restarted

### New direction (supersedes the four-version plan; start the next talk here)
- **bakeaweb.com** is the home of **BAKE Studio PRO**, **BAKE Studio BAKER**, plus bakedby.me, rezsite.com and bakedbya.pro.
- Only **two versions** of the Studio: one for **clients** (PRO) and one for **Bakers** (BAKER). EXPRESS and RezSite stop being Studio versions (what bakedby.me and rezsite.com become is still to be decided).
- Anyone can play with premium templates, sections and extensions for free. Items used go into a **cart**; the cart is paid when the user **publishes or downloads the code**.
- The **cart total** is always shown at the top right.

### Answers that probably still apply (confirm in the next talk)
- Pagenames: 3 to 12 characters; lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens; start with a letter.
- A pagename is unique **per domain** (three different people can own `sunrise` on bakedby.me, rezsite.com and bakedbya.pro).
- Sign-up: email/password **and** Google; asks for pagename, title and description.
- People can try the Studio without an account; the account is created at **Publish**.
- One **login** (the person) holds several **accounts** (one pagename = one **site**) and switches between them. 5 free accounts per login.
- A pagename can be renamed or deleted, never handed to someone else. On rename the old address can be kept and forwarded, but it uses one of the 5 accounts.
- Development can be handed to a Baker: the owner's editing is locked while they watch the work.
- Each pagename gets an email address and a GitHub repository (private preferred; private repos are free, only serving Pages from private repos needs a paid plan).
- Wording: "page" = one page, "site" = a website.
- Free sites keep the *Baked with BAKE* badge; removing it costs $5 one time.
- Hiring Bakers is for paying (PRO) clients only.
- Premium items: free users see them; trying them in PRO is screenshot mode; buying turns on the real code. Card verification was planned for PRO.
- PRO includes AI images (AI video later).
- BAKER: hub for four markets (services, templates, sections, extensions); Bakers practise in the Studio and submit templates, sections and extensions for approval.
- RezSite (free Indigenous sites): instant self-declaration at account creation, checks only if someone reports a concern; reviewed by the owner; non-Indigenous organizations serving Indigenous communities are welcome.
